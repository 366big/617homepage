KINGDOM PRINCIPLES

Unity · Balance · Teamwork · Peace

A kingdom built to compete together — without turning the battlefield against our own people.

01
⚔

ONE KINGDOM

Server events are for everyone. All players are encouraged to participate in KVK, Dragon Empire and Internal Castle War as one kingdom. Rather than separating players by alliance strength, Server 617 aims to give every player a meaningful role when the server competes together.

02
🛡

PEACE WITHIN 617

No NAP is needed. City attacks inside Server 617 are prohibited, creating a clear and stable environment for every alliance. Players can grow, recruit and prepare for major events without worrying about being attacked by another alliance from the same server.

03
⛏

FAIR BATTLEFIELD

All-Out War is limited to resource-gathering sites. Cities remain protected, while controlled competition is still possible when players want to contribute to server activities. The goal is to keep internal competition from damaging the cooperation we need for major events.

04
♟

SHARED STRATEGY

Leaders from every alliance share strategy, battle plans and feedback before and after major server events. Important decisions are discussed across alliances so that experience from one group can be shared with the entire kingdom. Every event becomes an opportunity to improve together.

05
◈

ONE TEAM, DIFFERENT ALLIANCES

Alliances keep their own identity, members and leadership while working toward the same kingdom objective. Strength is coordinated, not concentrated. Different alliances can have different roles, but when Server 617 enters a major event, the focus is on what benefits the kingdom as a whole.

06
✦

ESTABLISHED

Founded on July 12, 2025. Since the beginning, Server 617 has been built around cooperation between alliances and a peaceful internal environment.
